ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Maria Valentine

Maria Valentine writes for readers who have loved, lost, questioned, survived, healed, and found the courage to begin again.

Her work centers on the quiet places of the heart — the space between pain and peace, questions and faith, heartbreak and hope. Through reflective writing, personal storytelling, and words of encouragement, Maria creates books intended to remind readers that healing can happen one page, one day, and one choice at a time.

Maria Valentine is a pen name. For now, the words — not the face behind them — remain at the center of the journey.
